## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks whether loyalty means taking a side, listening, truthfulness, fairness, support, neutrality, or disagreement without abandonment.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- loyalty and truth;
- support and agreement;
- neutrality and responsibility;
- fairness and friendship.

## Defensible Positions

- Support a friend because friendship includes standing with someone in conflict.
- Correct or question a friend because support does not require defending a false claim.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Support versus agreement; neutrality versus fairness; correcting versus abandoning; listening versus taking sides.

## P4C Structure

The mission includes concrete conflict stimulus, initial judgment, reasons, perspectives, counterexample, reflection, and open ending.

## Perspective Quality

Perspectives avoid caricature and make both loyalty and fairness understandable.

## Counterexample Quality

The changed case tests whether a friend's false or incomplete claim changes the duty of support.

## Option Quality

Options do not make neutrality always ideal or loyalty automatically blind.

## Final Reflection Quality

Completion allows children to retain or revise views about support without judgment.

## Persian-Language Review

## Child-Safety Review

## Inclusion And Cultural Review

## Artwork Alignment
