The mission asks how promise, trust, changed circumstances, explanation, repair, and unreasonable promises affect what loyalty requires.

- trust and repair;

## Defensible Positions

- Keep the promise because promises have force even when inconvenient.
- Renegotiate or repair because the original promise did not include the changed condition.
