## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks whether a rule is fair merely because it applies equally, or whether safety, proportionality, exceptions, and respectful criticism can matter.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- equal application and proportionality;
- safety and freedom;
- simplicity and exception;
- authority and respectful criticism.

## Defensible Positions

- Keep the rule because simple equal rules can protect safety.
- Revise or question the rule because equal treatment can still be unfair in different circumstances.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Legal rule versus just rule; equal enforcement versus proportional response; criticism versus rule-breaking.

## P4C Structure
