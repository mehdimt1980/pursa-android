## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks how a group should think about equal membership, visible effort, invisible work, circumstance, and group identity when sharing recognition.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- equal membership and unequal effort;
- visible work and invisible work;
- circumstance and responsibility;
- agreement and justice.

## Defensible Positions

- Share equally because the prize belongs to the group.
- Consider different contributions and circumstances because equal shares may hide important differences.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Contribution versus membership; effort versus result; excuse versus relevant circumstance; agreement versus fairness.

## P4C Structure

The mission includes concrete group stimulus, judgment, reasons, perspectives, hidden-work/changed-condition testing, reflection, and open completion.

## Perspective Quality

Perspectives avoid labeling one child lazy and allow invisible work to matter without revealing a single answer.

## Counterexample Quality

The changed case tests whether visible contribution is the only relevant measure.
