## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks whether truth-telling, responsibility, intention, consequence, and trust still matter when a child did not mean to cause harm but another person may be blamed.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- honesty and self-protection;
- intention and consequence;
- responsibility and fear;
- trust and repair.

## Defensible Positions

- Tell the truth soon because another person may be blamed and trust depends on honesty.
- First look for a careful way to repair the harm because fear and lack of bad intention matter.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Truth versus repair; accident versus responsibility; silence versus lying; confession versus taking responsibility.

## P4C Structure

## Perspective Quality

The perspective is charitable because it shows why fear and intention might matter without making silence obviously right.

## Counterexample Quality
