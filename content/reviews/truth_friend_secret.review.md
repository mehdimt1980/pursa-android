## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks when loyalty and secrecy should yield to fairness, repair, and responsible truth-telling in a low-risk friendship situation.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- loyalty and fairness;
- privacy and repair;
- silence and responsible disclosure;
- trust and harm.

## Defensible Positions

- Keep the secret at first because friendship and trust matter.
- Seek a private repair path because silence may unfairly affect another child.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Secret versus harmful secrecy; gossip versus responsible disclosure; public exposure versus private repair.
