## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks how inclusion, group autonomy, repeated exclusion, practical capacity, friendship responsibility, and compromise should be balanced.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- inclusion and group autonomy;
- repeated exclusion and one-time limits;
- capacity and belonging;
- pity and friendship.

## Defensible Positions

- The group may keep boundaries because friendship and play cannot be fully forced.
- Repeated exclusion needs response because patterns can become unfair even when one instance is understandable.

## Conceptual Distinctions

Inclusion versus forced friendship; repeated exclusion versus limited capacity; pity versus respect; compromise versus obligation.
