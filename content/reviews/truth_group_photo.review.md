## Central Philosophical Problem

The mission asks whether a real image can still be unfair or misleading when context, caption, privacy, consent, and group memory are considered.

## Competing Values Or Concepts

- group memory and individual privacy;
- majority preference and consent;
- truth and context.

## Defensible Positions
