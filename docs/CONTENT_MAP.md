## Domain Balance

Truth strengths: responsibility, privacy, evidence, secrecy. Possible future gaps: definition of truth, ambiguity, honest mistake.

Justice strengths: distribution, representation, rules, contribution. Possible future gaps: punishment, restorative justice, equal opportunity.

Friendship strengths: belonging, promise, loyalty, exclusion. Possible future gaps: forgiveness, changing friendships, boundaries.
