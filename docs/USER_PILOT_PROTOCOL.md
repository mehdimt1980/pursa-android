# User Pilot Protocol

This is a future research and usability protocol. It is not implemented in the app and does not authorize collecting personal data.

## Purpose

- evaluate usability;
- observe comprehension;
- identify confusing wording;
- identify navigation difficulty;
- evaluate whether stories invite reasoning.

## Participants

- small pilot;
- children within the intended age range;
- parent or guardian consent;
- age-appropriate assent;
- no coercive recruitment.

## Data Minimization

Do not collect by default:

- full name;
- email;
- phone number;
- exact birthday;
- home address;
- school name;
- account identifier;
- audio;
- video;
- photo;
- free-text private disclosure;
- psychological profile.

Use anonymous participant codes only when genuinely necessary for research records. Do not add participant IDs to the app.

## Procedure

1. short introduction;
2. one mission;
3. observation of navigation;
4. neutral follow-up questions;
5. optional second mission;
6. debrief.

## Neutral Follow-Up Questions

- Which part was unclear?
- At which step did you need to think more?
- Was there an option whose meaning you did not understand?
- Did it seem like more than one answer could be defended?
- Did the story picture fit the events?

Avoid questions such as:

- What was the correct answer?
- Did you learn that you should always tell the truth?
- Have you ever done this yourself?
- What secret do you have?

## Researcher Notes

Record only:

- navigation confusion;
- wording confusion;
- task completion;
- apparent reading difficulty;
- questions asked;
- technical defects.

Do not record psychological interpretation.

## Recording Policy

Initial pilot should use written observer notes. Do not use audio, video, photos, or screen recording unless separately reviewed and consented.

## Retention

Use minimal retention, secure storage, a deletion schedule, no upload into the production app, and no linking to personal identity.

## Safeguarding

The child may stop at any time. There is no pressure to answer. Do not discuss private secrets. Parent or guardian procedures must be defined before the pilot. If distress occurs, stop the session, support the child, and follow the supervising adult and reviewer protocol.

This document alone does not satisfy legal, institutional, or ethics-board requirements. Institutional review may be required depending on the research context.
