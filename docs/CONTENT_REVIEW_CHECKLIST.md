# Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every future production story. Review is required, not optional.

## Content Identity

- stable story ID;
- world ID;
- content revision;
- title;
- summary;
- age range;
- duration;
- artwork key.

## Philosophical Quality

- central philosophical question;
- two or more defensible positions;
- real conceptual distinction;
- reasons requested;
- perspective charitable;
- counterexample meaningful;
- reflection open;
- no correct answer.

## P4C Flow

- concrete stimulus;
- initial judgment;
- reasons;
- alternative viewpoint;
- changed case;
- reconsideration;
- open ending.

## Language

- age appropriate;
- natural Persian;
- Persian characters;
- nim-faseleh where needed;
- concise options;
- no academic prose;
- no baby talk.

## Safety

- no personal disclosure;
- no shame;
- no fear-based coercion;
- no severe harm;
- no dangerous secrecy;
- no public humiliation.

## Inclusion

- no stereotype;
- no forced friendship;
- no blind loyalty;
- no blind obedience;
- no authority caricature;
- no identity coding.

## Artwork

- original;
- registered;
- neutral;
- no moral cue;
- no embedded text;
- accessibility classification;
- compact-size review.

## Technical

- parser;
- validator;
- manifest;
- artwork registry;
- Journal candidates;
- persistence compatibility;
- previews;
- tests;
- debug build;
- release build.

## Approval

- content review;
- Persian editorial review;
- safety review;
- artwork review;
- accessibility review;
- final production status.
