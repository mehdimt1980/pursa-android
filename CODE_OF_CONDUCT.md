# Code of Conduct

`پرسا | Pursa` is an open-source educational project for children. Contributors are expected to communicate with care, patience, and respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming to people from different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions when needed.
- Respect privacy, especially where children and families are concerned.
- Help keep discussions safe, inclusive, and useful.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Dismissing safety, accessibility, privacy, or child-protection concerns.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that conflicts with this code, raise it with the project maintainers through the appropriate repository channel. Reports should be handled with discretion and care.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and community communication related to `پرسا | Pursa`.
